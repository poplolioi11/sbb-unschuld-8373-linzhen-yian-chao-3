# 臨診醫案抄之三（柏林 Slg. Unschuld 8373） / Lin zhen yi an chao zhi san [Hand-copied medical cases with clinical diagnosis III]: Transcription Dataset

《臨診醫案抄之三》是柏林國家圖書館 Slg. Unschuld 8373 藏晚清漢文醫案抄本。逐案保存求診者稱謂、就診日期、症候、診察、擬方與再診文字，部分原葉背面仍留診案及旁注。可研究病例的書寫、追診和原稿使用。

Lin zhen yi an chao zhi san, Staatsbibliothek zu Berlin Slg. Unschuld 8373, is a late Qing manuscript of individual clinical cases. Entries record patient designations, dates, symptoms, observations and treatment or follow-up. Several verso faces also preserve independent notes.

111 幅均建立來源定位。正面、反面實書、反透、朱印與殘墨分層。

All 111 scans are indexed. Writing on both faces, mirrored show-through, red marks and unresolved traces are distinguished.

AI-assisted initial transcription with source references and marked uncertainties. Human full-text double proofreading, expert review, peer review and accuracy measurement were not performed. The transcription follows the manuscript right to left. Original forms and uncertain readings are retained. Show-through is recorded with the paired leaf.
